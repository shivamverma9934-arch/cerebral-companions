🎯 ERP Learning & Practice

Learn about Exposure and Response Prevention.

📘

What is ERP?

ERP is a specialised form of cognitive behavioural therapy used for OCD.

  • Exposure involves gradually facing a planned trigger.
  • Response prevention means practising not performing the usual compulsion.
  • A qualified OCD therapist can help plan suitable exercises.
